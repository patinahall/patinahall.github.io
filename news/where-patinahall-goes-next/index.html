Direction

Next: a listing that tells you what the piece actually is

Two things we are building. A piece for sale should carry the research PatinaHall has already done about it. And you should be able to ask to be told when the thing you are looking for appears.

Published 30 October 2026

The piece in front of you has a story already written

Open a listing for a Gispen 412 and you get photographs, a price, measurements and the shop. You do not get the Gispen 412 story — how that tubular outline carries upholstery, leaves the space open and gives a little as you sit — although it is written and sitting on this site in three languages. Read the story, and nothing tells you that one is for sale.

The gap runs both ways. A chrome-framed chair in the catalogue has a Guide about exactly what tarnish, pitting and plating loss can and cannot tell you, and the listing never mentions it.

We are closing it in both directions. A piece for sale should carry what PatinaHall knows about it — the object's history where we have researched it, and the Guide that matches the thing you would actually be inspecting. A story should say when you can buy one.

Being told when one appears

Retail assumes there is another one. Vintage does not. Hesitate over a sideboard and it is gone, and arriving on the wrong week means the piece you wanted simply is not here.

So the second thing: tell us what you are looking for, and we will tell you when one turns up. A standing request rather than a search you have to remember to repeat. For the shops, the same thing read from the other side — a truthful account of what people came looking for and did not find.

And the plainer work

Alongside those: a proper way to narrow the catalogue to the size, material and price you have room and budget for; delivery treated as a fact on the page rather than only as the subject of a Guide, because getting a wardrobe up a staircase is where these decisions are usually won or lost; and condition described the same way on every piece — what the shop observed, and what remains unknown.
