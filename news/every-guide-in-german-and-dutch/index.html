Language update

PatinaHall's 325 buying guides are now available in Dutch and German

All 325 guides are published in English, Dutch and German, each language at its own address. The first French guides are live, with a language control that keeps your choice as you browse.

Published 30 October 2026

325 guides, three languages

Every PatinaHall buying guide can now be read in Dutch and German as well as English. That is 325 guides in each language, each at its own address with its own copy rather than an English page behind a translated URL.

A Dutch or German address that does not exist behaves like any other missing page, instead of quietly serving English.

French has started

The first French guides are published, with a language control that keeps your choice as you move around the site. They cover what a French-speaking buyer tends to need first: the Wishbone and Shell chairs, the J39, the Barcelona, the Camaleonda, a sagging sofa, upholstery and leather, black stains in old wood, and what restoration is likely to cost before the piece is bought.

Two limitations worth knowing

A number of older technical diagrams still carry their English lettering, even on a Dutch or German page. The drawn diagrams inside the guides are translated; those photographic ones are not yet.

The Furniture Library is translated collection by collection rather than all at once, so parts of it are still English only, and it cannot be read in French yet.
