New shop

Nordlys Retro Hygge joins PatinaHall

A shop in Denmark has joined PatinaHall, with more than eighty pieces now on its own shop page. Its catalogue came in from its Etsy shop — the first time we have brought one in that way.

Published 30 October 2026

A welcome

Nordlys Retro Hygge, a shop in Denmark, has joined PatinaHall. Its page introduces the shop in its own words before the catalogue begins, and more than eighty of its pieces are there now with prices, measurements and photographs.

The best way to meet a shop is to look at what it chose to carry, so that is where we will leave you.

Brought in from an Etsy shop

Every shop here has so far arrived through its own website — a storefront PatinaHall recognised and read. This catalogue came from the shop's Etsy shop instead, through the ordinary import rather than a job built for the occasion.

That opens the door for a lot of dealers. Plenty of independent vintage sellers never built a website: they work from an Etsy shop and a phone full of photographs. If joining PatinaHall meant rebuilding a catalogue somewhere else first, they would not join, and the furniture would not get found.
