Store update

Every PatinaHall store now introduces the people behind it

Every store in the PatinaHall directory now has a published profile: a portrait, a logo and a personal introduction from the people who find, repair and sell the furniture.

Published 15 October 2026

A shop is a person with a point of view

A good vintage shop is not a warehouse. Someone decides which piece is worth carrying down a narrow staircase, which repair is honest and which patina should be left exactly as it is. Those decisions are the reason two shops selling the same era feel nothing alike.

Until now a store could arrive on PatinaHall as little more than a name above a grid of products. Every store in the directory now has a profile of its own, so a buyer can meet the shop before deciding to trust one of its pieces.

A portrait, a logo and words of their own

Each profile leads with the shop's own photograph and introduction rather than a summary of its stock. One store is shown through its restoration workshop, another through the people who run it, another through the furniture as it waits to be collected.

Logos and introductions carry into the store directory as well, so browsing shops means reading a row of distinct places instead of a list of labels. Every image and every sentence is published with the shop's agreement.

Why this matters for buyers

Buying something old is a decision about a specific object in the care of a specific person. Knowing who prepared a piece, where they look for furniture and what they refuse to sell tells a buyer something that no condition report can.

PatinaHall is a marketplace for vintage furniture, and this is how we bring buyers to the people who found the piece, not just to a photograph of it.
