Preparing for payments: fees, delivery and seller payouts explained

Planned, not yet available. PatinaHall does not take payment for a piece today. This is the written explanation of how it will work, published before the feature so sellers can read it in advance.

Published 30 October 2026

Planned, not yet available

PatinaHall does not take payment for a piece today. A sale is still arranged directly between the buyer and the shop. What is published now is the explanation of how paying will work, written down before the feature exists so sellers can read it in advance rather than during their first sale.

What the explanation covers

A seller's payment account and why it belongs to the shop rather than to PatinaHall. The order of events, from a buyer's first message through the agreed piece and the agreed delivery price to the payment itself. A worked example with real numbers: the piece, the delivery, what the buyer pays, which fees are deducted, what reaches the shop's own balance and when it is paid out to their bank.

It also names the four parties separately — the buyer, the shop, PatinaHall and the payment provider — because they carry different responsibilities and reading them as one thing is how people end up surprised.

What is still to come

The buyer side of the same explanation, in the buying FAQ. Dutch and German versions. And an update to both when payments actually launch, so that the wording and the working feature agree.
