Store update

Entourage Finds is now featured on PatinaHall

A dedicated Entourage Finds Store page now brings its current furniture and object selection together on PatinaHall.

Published 9 August 2026

Today we published a dedicated Store page for Entourage Finds, bringing its current furniture and object selection together on PatinaHall.

Entourage Finds is a Netherlands family business that grew from collecting vintage furniture and art for its own home. Its selection focuses on twentieth-century European design chosen for quality, character, and lasting appeal.

One place to explore the current collection

The new Store page introduces Entourage Finds and provides a direct path into the pieces currently presented through PatinaHall, with prices and availability kept alongside each object.

It is another small step towards making independent stores and memorable objects easier to discover.
