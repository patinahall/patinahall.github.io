PatinaHall can now bring in nopCommerce catalogues

Vintage furniture shops running a nopCommerce storefront can now bring their existing catalogue and product photography into PatinaHall, while the original shop stays the source.

Published 15 October 2026

One more platform we read instead of asking you to retype

A shop that already runs its own online store has done the hard work once: photographed every piece, described it, priced it. Asking that shop to do it again for PatinaHall means maintaining two catalogues, and the second one is stale within a month.

nopCommerce storefronts now join the platforms PatinaHall can read directly, alongside the catalogue imports already in use. A nopCommerce shop's products — and the photography published with them — can be brought in and offered to buyers here.

Your shop stays the source

Connecting a storefront does not publish anything. The catalogue is prepared first, and the owner decides which pieces are added and completes the ordinary review for anything buyers will see.

When the shop changes, the import can be run again: new and changed pieces are brought forward for review, and a piece keeps the same public address on PatinaHall, so a link shared last month still opens the right object today.

Less catalogue admin, more time on the floor

PatinaHall is a marketplace for vintage furniture built around zero maintenance for the shop: you keep running the store you already have, and we keep its presence here current.

If your shop runs on another platform, tell us which one. The list of catalogues we can read directly is still growing.
