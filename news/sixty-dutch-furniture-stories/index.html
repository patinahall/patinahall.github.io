Forty-five objects and fifteen designers

The Furniture Library has sixty new stories about Dutch furniture: forty-five objects and fifteen profiles of the designers and makers behind them. All sixty were published in English, Dutch and German on the same day.

Where to start

Gerrit Rietveld kept asking what the cheapest honest answer looked like. The Crate Chair was built from ordinary packing boards and sold on a completely different bargain. The Zig-Zag Chair is four wooden planes, and the whole difficulty is hidden at its corners.

Then there is the factory thread, which is less famous and just as interesting. Gispen 412 is a tubular outline that carries upholstery and gives a little as you sit. Look beneath the familiar Result school chair and you find shaped sheet steel. Auping's Cleopatra made a single bed restrained enough to belong in a room used all day.

And the material thread. Maarten Baas burned furniture and kept what survived the fire. Piet Hein Eek made the leftovers the point. Jurgen Bey's Tree Trunk Bench notices that in most places the seat is already there. Dirk Vander Kooij's Endless is a chair drawn in one thick line of reclaimed plastic.

Among the profiles: Friso Kramer, Hella Jongerius, Gijs Bakker, Kho Liang Ie, Renny Ramakers and Piet Hein Eek.
